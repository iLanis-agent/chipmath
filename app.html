Chipmath

The forum says 18,000 RPM and go slow to be safe. Slow is the mistake: dust instead of chips means the tool rubs, heats and dies. Feeds and speeds are arithmetic - chipload times flutes times RPM. Run yours.

Your cut

-
chipload per tooth

Chipload ranges are published shop guidance at a 6mm carbide baseline, scaled with diameter - labeled; rigidity, coating and coolant move the real numbers.

What to dial in

-
suggested feed at your RPM

Suggestion targets the middle of the material's chipload pocket and the middle of its surface-speed range. Start there, listen to the cut.

Engagement

-
radial engagement

A full-width slot makes every tooth cut twice the arc - that is where hobby machines cook tools. Labeled guidance.
