The problem it solves

Forum settings travel without context: that magic 18,000 RPM came from a different machine, a different tool, a different material. The number that matters is chipload - how much each tooth bites - and it is one multiplication away: feed rate divided by flutes divided by RPM.

Chipmath computes your chipload and surface speed, grades them against published material guidance, suggests the feed for your spindle, and flags the full-width slot before it cooks the tool. Exact arithmetic, labeled guidance.
